Conditional support for a fresh common strict-FP32 campaign, not numerical certification of the current package. Do not launch dependent scientific jobs until precision enforcement and the full-batch gates below pass. Keep all original scientific solver settings and the common historical backward/density implementation. A real hard row already survives FP64, so “strict FP32 fixes convergence” is too strong.

This review read the revision2 solver, runner, evaluator, audit and both test files, the local original engine, and the raw GPU/CPU results, conditioning, finite-difference and telemetry JSON. No server actions, source changes, training or GPU work were performed. Findings are source inspection and independent extraction of existing diagnostics, not a new reproduction of those diagnostics.

## Solver algebra and semantics

The local equation F_k(x)=x-previous-dt*v(x,obs,t_k), its Jacobian I-dt*Dv, and solve J*p=-F have the correct signs. Sequential equations are algebraically equivalent to the joint cumulative implicit-Euler fixed point. Equivalence concerns exact roots, not identical finite-budget iterates or branch choices.

Armijo uses phi=||F||²/2 and slope FᵀJp, accepts only finite candidates with sufficient decrease, and tries alpha=1 through 2^-12. A failed line search retains the last accepted iterate. Active-row filtering, per-step status storage, and all_converged across all four steps are coherent. Convergence at the iteration cap is correctly distinct from failure. A preceding local failure remains visible while later steps solve relative to its returned iterate. Jacobian field-position accounting includes the primal evaluation, while jacobian_evals records extra AD work separately. Work counts are not equivalent FLOP costs.

One important limitation: stored residuals were evaluated at the active-subset shape and are never universally recomputed before reporting convergence. A later flattened B*T evaluation can differ. Thus native status and independently recomputed common residual must remain separate quantities. Strict FP32 reduces this issue but does not remove it, as the pilot's one discrepant row shows. Do not silently change stopping tolerances to make both pass.

A secondary defensive weakness is that finite components can have overflowed norms or merits. classify checks finite x/F but not finite norms/scale, so an infinity-versus-infinity comparison could falsely accept huge finite values. No provided artifact shows that regime. Make finite norm, merit and scale a preflight assertion and preserve evidence if encountered. Do not claim comprehensive nonfinite protection from the current tests.

The audit's local_relative denominator is 1+||previous||, while Newton's rel_residual denominator is max(||x||,||previous||). They are different metrics. Compare absolute residual against the explicit common tolerance or add an explicitly named normalized criterion. Do not compare those relative residual fields as interchangeable.

## Backward and density scope

The runner replaces the forward solve while retaining native attachment r=g(q). Parameter derivatives are evaluated through g at q, while the dense shifted adjoint is formed using field Jacobians at r. The matrix is (I-dt*M*blockdiag(Dv(r)))ᵀ+1e-6*I. This is historical mixed-point implicit differentiation, not differentiation of Newton iterations and not an exact unshifted equilibrium derivative away from convergence. Training receives r, while no-grad rollout receives q. Measure both trajectories and attachment action drift.

The audit mirrors this attachment, shift, density safeguards and optional SAC loss, and its small FP64 parity test is valuable. It does not prove all production full-batch FP32 gradients are correct. In particular, a finite-difference derivative of a finite stopping/line-search program should not be used as a mandatory equality gate for an implicit surrogate gradient.

Density uses shifted local determinants, determinant clamps, tanh and action-scale guards, plus training logp rails. Strict precision changes their numerical values without changing their formulas. Validated local roots with nonsingular Jacobians do not establish global injectivity, unique roots, correct root-branch probabilities or exact likelihood of the finite sampler. The independent FP64 reference validates residual <=1e-9 and sigma_min>1e-10, regardless of SciPy success flag. That is reasonable equation-based acceptance, but not an error bound near singularity. Inspect conditioning and branch distances. The audit correctly withholds full-batch reference-gradient comparisons when any row lacks a validated reference. Never silently drop row473 and relabel the remaining batch's gradient as the original full-batch gradient.

## P0 blockers before restart

1. Common precision enforcement is currently absent. The original engine main explicitly sets CUDA matmul TF32 true, cuDNN TF32 true, and matmul precision high. Setting strict policy only before calling main is insufficient. Enforce highest/false/false after the legacy assignments and before any model work, and verify at actual solver, density/backward, evaluator and audit execution boundaries. Disable autocast and reject non-FP32 production actor/critic inputs and parameters. Preserve the source engine and version the integration change in a new package. Record requested and effective settings, Torch/CUDA/GPU identity, package hashes and environment overrides. Evaluation must restore the recorded policy and reject incompatible policy, not rely on fresh-process defaults.
2. Existing diagnostics do not resolve the strict GPU pilot's status-versus-residual discrepancy. Identify the row/step and quantitatively test whether it is an FP32 tolerance-boundary case. A blanket epsilon or relabeling native success as independently verified is unacceptable.
3. The release gate must distinguish known intrinsic solver failure from implementation failure. A rule demanding every row converges would fail the already-known A2 row in FP64. Preserve it as a required adversarial fixture and require honest status/finite retained iterate and independent failure classification. Conversely, do not waive a new strict-only hard failure merely because one historical hard row exists.

## Practical numerical preflight gates

Freeze selection and thresholds before observing new results. Use the three existing full batches plus one fresh seed-0 production-size actor-update capsule from each N/A/H/T pilot. Keep all rows, all four steps, actor/critics/noise/loss configuration and actual steps. Include both gradient-enabled attachment and no-grad use. Run on each assigned GPU at least the policy assertion, full-batch forward and one full actor/critic update, even if detailed CPU FP64 checks are shared across identical hardware.

1. **Precision and regression gate:** no skipped required tests, exact source/hash provenance, actual highest precision and both TF32 flags false, autocast off, float32 production tensors. Repeat assertions after engine initialization and checkpoint restoration. Run each N/A/H/T CUDA smoke and checkpoint evaluation. All actions, raw logp, losses, gradients, updated weights, solver trajectories, residual norms and scales must be finite. A guarded value being finite must not hide a nonfinite raw diagnostic.
2. **Full-batch residual gate:** save per-row/per-step native status, stored F, fresh F at B*T, fresh F at B per step, and FP64 reevaluation of the identical returned FP32 trajectory. Use tau=1e-7+1e-5*max(||q_k||,||q_(k-1)||), including z at the first step. Report ||F||/tau and exact masks with no relaxation. Check permutations and fixed chunks (1, 32, 1024) for the whole batch, not a first-32 prefix. Chunk-size changes are diagnostic only and must not alter the production solver.
3. **Boundary classification gate:** for every disagreement compute the actual residual differences across shapes and FP64 at fixed q, rather than comparing separately re-solved trajectories. A rounding-boundary label requires the threshold margin to be no larger than the measured residual-evaluation uncertainty, with a fixed small norm-rounding allowance such as 8*eps32*max(1,||q||,||previous||,dt*||v||). Also report this allowance divided by tau. If the allowance is a material part of tau (suggested escalation at 10%), classify as unresolved numerical sensitivity rather than claiming certified convergence. Check that the stored/native and recomputed scales agree. Keep exact failure counts even when a discrepancy is explained. No tolerance change is needed to pass an honestly classified boundary case.
4. **Hard-row gate:** every strict-only failure, every unexplained discrepancy, every nonfinite, and the known A2 row473 receive FP64 solve, local sigma_min/condition, residual-versus-iteration and alpha/Armijo trace, and independent SciPy validation. For a finite hard failure reproduced in FP64, retain native failure and downstream contamination indicators. It can pass the *implementation* gate without passing the *convergence* criterion. No hidden fallback, retries with relaxed tolerances, row removal, or substitution of a different reference branch. Any new unexplained failure blocks the affected launch until diagnosed. Valid reference at a different root requires a branch caveat, not an automatic solver-bug verdict.
5. **Jacobian/Armijo gate:** compute FP64 central differences at actual 2D local states over the full batch (only four field evaluations per local state for two coordinates, readily batched). Use several h values around 1e-6 relative to state scale, retaining the plateau error, and require relative matrix error <=1e-6 or absolute <=1e-7 in FP64 on smooth finite states. For all failing and selected normal rows log accepted steps and verify the actual Armijo inequality and linear-solve residual with the original evaluation shapes. Expect Jp+F small, scaled by ||J||*||p||+||F||, rather than demanding tiny absolute residual for ill-conditioned matrices.
6. **Production backward/density gate:** full batch native-versus-audit parity under the same frozen q, obs, z and SAC configuration, with identical historical attachment. Require finite outputs and parameter gradients. On well-conditioned cases use a declared FP32 allclose budget (suggestion atol 1e-6, rtol 1e-4 for readouts, normalized gradient difference <=1e-3), investigate breaches instead of widening after the fact. Report shifted adjoint residual and unshifted residual separately, plus raw/guarded determinant differences, logp clamps and attachment drift. Full-batch FP64 reference gradients are only available when all rows validate. Candidate-to-reference gradient disagreement is an experimental outcome, not necessarily an implementation failure.
7. **Timing gate:** rerun seed-0 8000-transition non-smoke pilots for all four arms. Measure post-learning-start elapsed with full batch 1024 and synchronize frozen timings, retaining warmups/repetitions. Passing timing means obtaining trustworthy throughput/ETA and resource feasibility, not beating Anderson. Include telemetry overhead and one-hard-row latency. Preserve these pilots outside scientific results.

The proposed numerical thresholds are review recommendations to preregister, not thresholds already demonstrated by the supplied artifacts. In particular, full-batch parity and strict boundary attribution remain unperformed here.

## P1 limitations and reporting requirements

The 16 tests cover important algebra, status and small-actor parity but do not exercise production neural-field CUDA shapes, dynamic subset drift, all hard rows, or precision-policy persistence. Add focused policy and boundary regression coverage in the new package, not a broad solver rewrite. Existing full-batch diagnostics are early N and middle A policy states, not late strict-trained N/H/T. Schedule predetermined periodic capsule checks during the new campaign. Keep separate native failure, common-threshold failure, explained boundary discrepancy, real FP64 hard failure, cap reached, and budget exhausted fields.

The restart is a changed numerical intervention for all four arms, including critics and backward operations. It is still a controlled forward-solver comparison within the new common policy, but cannot be pooled with the old TF32 campaign as if only performance engineering changed. Old partial results remain diagnostic evidence. Do not promise exact likelihood, exact finite-program gradients, universal convergence, equal wall-clock comparison, or the same learned policy after restart.
